You are a trader at a bank in Dubai with 1,000,000 dirhams. In each round, read the prices, decide if there is a riskless profit, then trade and see what you end with.

The dirham peg of 3.6725 dirhams per dollar is real. Every other price and interest rate on this page is made up for practice. Nothing you do on this page is saved or sent anywhere.
